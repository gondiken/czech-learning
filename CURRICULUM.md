# Curriculum roadmap

Learner: Turkish native, speaks English at home and work, lives in Czechia, has little time.
Goal: confident survival + friendly small talk, not exams. Practical before grammatical.

Design rules for every lesson
- 6–8 words max (numbers lessons may have 10). Every word gets an emoji, English and Turkish.
- Every lesson has one **Turkish bridge**: a sound, word or grammar idea Turkish already has
  (ç/ş/j letters, accusative -(y)i ≈ Czech -u, cases ≈ suffixes, siz/sen ≈ vy/ty, soft k/g ≈ ť/ď,
  shared loanwords: tramvaj/tramvay, šofér/şoför, autobus/otobüs, čaj/çay, kabát? check before using).
  Never invent a false similarity; if unsure, use a pronunciation or "false friend" tip instead.
- Dialogues come from real Czech life: shops (Albert, Lidl, Billa), pubs, trams, pharmacy, neighbours,
  post office / Zásilkovna, kids' school or školka, the foreign police (OAMP), the doctor.
- Recycle 2–3 words the learner missed recently (from `progress/main.log[].missed`) inside new dialogues.
- Line colours rotate like Prague metro lines: A (green), B (yellow), C (red), 6 lessons… roughly.

Done: stations 1–14 (greetings, survival, café, numbers, till, groceries, intro + cases, family,
big numbers, directions, transport, time words, pharmacy, small talk).
